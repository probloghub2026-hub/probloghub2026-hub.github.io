19. Understanding Blockchain Technology

Blockchain technology is a decentralized system for recording and verifying transactions across multiple computers. Unlike traditional databases, blockchain creates a secure and transparent record that is difficult to alter or tamper with. The technology gained popularity through cryptocurrencies, but its applications extend far beyond digital currencies. Businesses are exploring blockchain for supply chain management, identity verification and secure data sharing. Because records are distributed across a network, blockchain can improve security and reduce reliance on central authorities. Many industries are investing in blockchain research to discover new ways of improving efficiency and transparency. As adoption continues to grow, blockchain has the potential to transform how organizations manage information and conduct transactions in the digital economy.
