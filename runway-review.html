Runway ML is a powerful AI video generation and editing platform. It allows users to create cinematic videos using text prompts, images, and AI models. In 2026, it is widely used by filmmakers, content creators, and marketing teams.

Key Features

  • Text-to-video generation
  • AI video editing tools
  • Green screen removal (AI background removal)
  • Motion tracking & effects
  • High-quality cinematic output

Pros

Cons
